# Format: interactive scene

A world to explore with the camera, with no game goal. It uses the template with no variation: take [prompt-template.md](prompt-template.md) as it is.

## Format round

1. How does the person move? (free flight: WASD + mouse, Q/E up and down, Shift to speed up (recommended) / an automatic cinematic tour, with optional free camera / orbit around a point / walk on the ground)
2. Extras, any number or none: ambient sound generated by code / start menu with a loading screen / touch controls on phones / collision with the terrain.

Mention in half a line that each extra costs build time and performance.

## Where each answer goes in the template

- How they move: in the second sentence of the opening (`let me fly a free camera through it`, `let me watch a guided camera tour and take over at any time`, `let me orbit around it`, `let me walk through it`) and in the CONTROLS line. For a guided tour, the scripted 60 s path is the tour itself.
- Sound: a SCENE item (`Procedural ambient sound: …, starting on the first click`), and CONSTRAINTS keeps `no texture, model or sound files`.
- Menu, touch, collision: on the CONTROLS line after a semicolon (`…, Shift to accelerate; start menu with a loading bar; touch steering on phones; solid terrain you slide along`).
- Touch on phones also adds the graceful-degradation line to TARGET.

## Verification poses

Six: the hero frame; a wide view from the starting point; a landmark up close; looking into the main light; a close-up of a surface or material; the densest area.
