# Format: video

A sequence rendered with code alone and exported as a video file. Since it doesn't have to run in real time, each frame can spend more: supersampling, motion blur, more lighting steps. What gets judged is the beauty of each frame, the pacing and the absence of motion artifacts.

## Format round A: destination, length and sound

1. Where will the video play? (full screen or YouTube, 16:9 / Reels, TikTok or Shorts, 9:16 / square feed, 1:1 / a looping background for a website or a big screen)
2. How long is it, and does the end need to join the beginning? (15 s / 30 s / 60 s / 2 to 3 min) and (seamless loop / normal start and end)
3. Sound? (score and ambience generated by code, with cuts on the beat / ambience only / no sound, the person adds it later in editing)

## Format round B: the sequence

Propose a storyboard of 3 to 6 beats for the chosen concept. If the person sent a script, build on it. Each beat has:

- a time range (`0–8 s`);
- a shot (aerial view, wide shot, close-up, low angle…);
- a camera move (dolly, orbit, crane, fly-through, locked-off);
- what happens or changes (the light turns, something appears, a transformation, a reveal).

The hero frame must sit in one of these beats, ideally at the climax. Ask what to change and whether there will be text on screen (none / a title at the start / a title and end credits / the brand logo at the end). Text is drawn by code, with a system font or vector lettering.

## Variation in the template

The template in [prompt-template.md](prompt-template.md) applies in full, with these changes. Numbers are in [budgets.md](budgets.md), section "Video".

**Opening paragraph.** `Build a {duration} s {concept} video rendered entirely by code in the browser. It only needs to look beautiful and follow the sequence below, with every frame good enough to be a still.`

**PRIORITIES.** Default: `1. Visual quality 2. Clean motion (no flicker, shimmer or stutter in the export) 3. Render time`.

**CONSTRAINTS.** The four fixed lines, with `Ship a single self-contained index.html` becoming `Ship a single self-contained index.html (preview and render) plus the exported video`, and one more: `Deterministic: frame n is rendered at time n/{fps}; {export method from budgets.md}.`

**TARGET.**

```text
TARGET
- {OS}, {browser}, {GPU}, {CPU} (render machine)
- Output: {W×H} at {fps} fps, exactly {frames} frames, {codec and container}{, seamless loop}. {Quality: 2×2 supersampling | 2×2 supersampling plus 8-sample motion blur}. Full export in under {minutes} min; live preview averages ≤ 33 ms per frame at {half resolution}.
```

**SEQUENCE**, in place of CONTROLS:

```text
SEQUENCE
- {0–8 s}: {shot}, {camera move}, {what happens}
- {8–20 s}: …
- Sound: {procedural score at {BPM} BPM with cuts on the downbeat | ambience only | none}
- On-screen text: {none | title at the start | title and end credits | brand logo on the last {n} s}, drawn by code
```

**VERIFICATION.**

```text
VERIFICATION
- Visual critic: a separate subagent that only ever sees your renders, never your code or reasoning. Hand it one full-resolution still from the middle of each SEQUENCE beat plus the exported file, so it can check every beat, judge pacing and spot shimmer, aliasing, popping, banding and compression artifacts. It answers with concrete defects in priority order. Keep iterating until nothing it reports is worse than "minor".
- Technical check: inspect the file with ffprobe or similar (exact resolution, frame rate, duration and frame count, no dropped or duplicated frames{, last frame flows into the first}); render five sampled frames twice and compare hashes to prove determinism. Report the total render time.
```

**DONE WHEN.** `DONE WHEN: both verification gates pass. Deliver index.html, the video file, the render time, the technical report and the critic's final review.`
